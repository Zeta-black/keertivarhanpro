All values below are computational predictions. Nothing here has been tested in a laboratory. NO QUANTUM HARDWARE EXECUTION — the problem is expressed in a form a quantum annealer could accept, and that is the whole quantum claim. No peptide has been synthesised, and no binding, adhesion, plastic degradation or recycling benefit is demonstrated. Scores are a dimensionless selectivity proxy used to rank designs against one another inside one model.
